A Panorama-specific operating model for moving from disconnected People systems to a more connected employee lifecycle.

Current state · disconnected systems

  1. Candidate and offer data live in Workable.
  2. Accepted offer triggers manual handoff.
  3. People Operations reconciles information in ADP.
  4. Teams follow up across separate tools.
→

Potential future state · connected lifecycle

  1. Define source of truth and handoff point.
  2. Validate required employee fields.
  3. Route exceptions to named owners.
  4. Provide consistent downstream data.
Why I would evaluate Rippling Pro: Based on Panorama’s scope, Pro may be a better fit than Core because it could support multi-step workflow automation, custom reporting, and deeper cross-functional data connections. At Prezent, Core was limited to five workflows. I would validate plan requirements, pricing, permissions, and integrations before a final recommendation.

How I build.

The same approach applies whether the work is payroll, onboarding, compliance, or automation.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Map the real process

Find handoffs, manual work, ownership gaps, exceptions, and failure modes.

02 / REDESIGN

Make the path clear

Define source of truth, approvals, controls, escalation routes, and human-led decisions.

03 / AUTOMATE

Measure the leverage

Connect tools and track time saved, errors prevented, and adoption.

Responsible by design

Speed with safeguards.

  • PII minimized by default
  • Human approval for sensitive decisions
  • Clear owners and escalation paths
  • Audit trails and source documentation
  • Exception handling, not forced automation
