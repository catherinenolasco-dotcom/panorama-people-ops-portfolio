A Panorama-specific operating model for moving from disconnected People systems to a more connected employee lifecycle.

Current state · disconnected systems

WorkableADPCulture Amp
  1. Candidate and offer data live in Workable.
  2. Accepted offer triggers a manual handoff or export.
  3. People Operations re-enters or reconciles information in ADP.
  4. TA, People, Payroll, Finance, and IT follow up across separate tools.
  5. Missing or inconsistent fields are often found downstream.
→

Future state · connected employee lifecycle

WorkableRipplingCulture Amp
  1. Define the source of truth and handoff point for each field.
  2. Validate required job, manager, location, start-date, and employment fields.
  3. Create the employee record in the approved future-state system.
  4. Route exceptions to the right owner with a visible review trail.
  5. Provide consistent downstream data for onboarding, payroll, reporting, and compliance.
Design principle: redesign the handoff first, then use integration and automation to reduce duplicate entry, surface exceptions earlier, and preserve human review.
WorkableRecruiting and offer-stage data.
RipplingPotential employee system of record and lifecycle workflows.
ADP / payrollCurrent-state validation and transition planning.
Culture AmpEmployee listening and feedback-to-action tracking.

How I build.

The same approach applies whether the work is payroll, onboarding, compliance, or automation.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Map the real process

Find handoffs, manual work, ownership gaps, exceptions, and failure modes before changing the system.

02 / REDESIGN

Make the path clear

Define source of truth, approval points, controls, escalation routes, and what remains human-led.

03 / AUTOMATE

Measure the leverage

Connect tools, document the workflow, and track time saved, errors prevented, and adoption.

Responsible by design

Speed with safeguards.

  • PII minimized by default
  • Human approval for sensitive decisions
  • Clear owners and escalation paths
  • Audit trails and source documentation
  • Exception handling, not forced automation
  • Fictional data for portfolio demos
