# Panorama People Operations Portfolio

A Panorama Education-inspired case study by Cat Nolasco, focused on People Operations systems, employee lifecycle design, and responsible AI-enabled coordination.

## What this demonstrates

- Discovery before solution design
- Current-state and illustrative future-state workflow thinking
- Workable, ADP, Culture Amp, and planned Rippling context
- HRIS, payroll, compliance, lifecycle, and cross-functional operating experience
- Human approval and PII safeguards for sensitive People decisions
- A synthetic employee-change intake prototype

## Important note

This is a candidate-created portfolio case study, not an official Panorama Education site or implementation proposal. The future-state architecture is illustrative and subject to discovery and validation. The demo uses synthetic data only and does not connect to a live HRIS or make employment decisions.
